<h3><strong>What do I need to join the Passion+ PLP?</strong></h3>
<p><strong>Passion+ PLP (Entrepreneurship):</strong>
<br>A strong passion for starting your own business! You can join with a pre-formed
team or find like-minded teammates during the programme.</p>
<p><strong>Passion+ PLP (SEA Readiness):</strong>
<br>Bring your adaptability, curiosity, cultural sensitivity, and open-mindedness.
This track is for those keen on exploring opportunities in Southeast Asia.</p>
<hr>
<h3><strong>What kind of exclusive opportunities does Passion+ PLP offer?</strong></h3>
<p><strong>Entrepreneurship Track:</strong>
</p>
<ul>
<li>
<p>Green Lane access to the <strong>Accelerator Programme</strong> and <strong>Global Entrepreneurial Internship Programme (GEIP)</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Personalised mentorship from an <strong>NP mentor</strong> and <strong>industry professionals</strong>.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>SEA Readiness Track:</strong>
</p>
<ul>
<li>
<p>Priority consideration for overseas programmes such as:</p>
<ul>
<li>
<p><strong>TF-SCALE</strong> (Temasek Foundation Specialists’ Community Action
and Leadership Exchange)</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>SAIL</strong> (South-east Asia Immersion Leadership)</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>GEIP</strong> (Global Entrepreneurial Internship Programme)</p>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<p>Additional priority for <strong>Youth Expedition Programme</strong> and <strong>Overseas Immersion Programme</strong>.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<hr>
<h3><strong>When does the programme start and what is the commitment like?</strong></h3>
<ul>
<li>
<p>Express interest in <strong>Semester 1 of Year 1</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Begin reading Learning Units (LUs) from <strong>Semester 2</strong> onwards.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Time commitment:</strong>
</p>
<ul>
<li>
<p>~3 hours per week for LUs.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Internship lasts up to <strong>6 months in Year 3</strong>.</p>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Entrepreneurship Track:</strong> Timeline is flexible and guided
by mentors.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Some endorsed external programmes may occur <strong>outside curriculum hours</strong>.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<hr>
<h3><strong>What are the costs for overseas trips?</strong></h3>
<ul>
<li>
<p><strong>TF-SCALE</strong> and <strong>SAIL</strong>: Airfare and programme
fees <strong>fully subsidised</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Youth Expedition Programme (YEP)</strong> and <strong>Overseas Immersion Programme (OIP)</strong>:
One-time <strong>subsidy</strong> for Singaporeans and PRs.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Students bear <strong>personal expenses</strong> (e.g. meals, shopping).</p>
</li>
</ul>
<hr>
<h3><strong>What are the costs for overseas internships and GEIP?</strong></h3>
<ul>
<li>
<p>These are <strong>award programmes</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Subsidies</strong> provided for:</p>
<ul>
<li>
<p>Airfare</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Accommodation</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Visa fees</p>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<p>Students cover a portion of <strong>living expenses abroad</strong>.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<hr>
<h3><strong>Will I get credit exemptions?</strong></h3>
<p>No formal credit exemptions.
<br>However, students in the <strong>Entrepreneurship track</strong> may replace
the <strong>Accelerator or Incubation LU</strong> under the Minor in Entrepreneurship
with endorsed external competitions or programmes.</p>
<hr>
<h3><strong>Do I need to give up equity in my start-up?</strong></h3>
<p>No, <strong>NP does not take any equity</strong> in businesses you start
under the programme.</p>
