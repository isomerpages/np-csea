<h3><strong>SAIL Programme – Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)</strong></h3>
<h4><strong>1. Can a Year 3 Final semester student apply?</strong></h4>
<p>Yes, <strong>Year 3 final semester students</strong> are eligible to apply.
We welcome applications from students across different years of study who
meet the programme criteria.</p>
<h4><strong>2. Do I need to have a leadership position in my CCA to apply?</strong></h4>
<p>No, <strong>a leadership position is not compulsory</strong>. However,
we are interested in hearing about your <strong>CCA participation and experiences</strong>.</p>
<h4><strong>3. Is there a cost to participate in the SAIL programme?</strong></h4>
<p>This is an <strong>award programme</strong>, and the <strong>cost of the trip is fully sponsored by Ngee Ann Polytechnic (NP)</strong>.
<br>This applies to <strong>both Singapore Citizens and Singapore PR students</strong>.</p>
<h4><strong>4. Will the trip be subsidised or do we pay any part of it?</strong></h4>
<p>No payment is required. The <strong>trip is fully covered</strong> by NP
under this award programme.</p>
<h4><strong>5. How many students will be selected for the trip?</strong></h4>
<p>There are <strong>30 places</strong> available for the SAIL programme in <strong>March 2025</strong>.
<br>All applications will be assessed <strong>on their own merit</strong>,
regardless of competition.</p>
<h4><strong>6. What are the selection criteria for the interview?</strong></h4>
<p>We are looking for students who have:</p>
<ul>
<li>
<p>Participated in a <strong>School Trip, Overseas Immersion Programme (OIP)</strong>,
or <strong>Youth Expedition Programme (YEP)</strong> in a Southeast Asian
city.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Strong <strong>teamwork</strong> and <strong>cross-cultural collaboration</strong> skills.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Adaptability</strong> and <strong>problem-solving</strong> abilities.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>A sense of <strong>curiosity</strong> and <strong>innovation</strong>.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<h4><strong>7. When will the trip take place?</strong></h4>
<p>The trip is <strong>tentatively scheduled from 17–30 March 2025</strong>.
Final dates will be confirmed with selected students.</p>
<h4><strong>8. Are there any preparatory requirements before the trip?</strong></h4>
<p>Yes, selected students must attend a <strong>1-day pre-trip session</strong> before
departure.</p>
<h4><strong>9. Are there any deliverables or commitments after the programme?</strong></h4>
<p>Yes, the programme is conducted in a <strong>hackathon format</strong>,
and the key deliverable will be a <strong>group showcase presentation</strong> at
the end of the programme in each city.</p>
