<h3><strong>SAIL: Southeast Asia Immersion Leadership Programme</strong></h3>
<p><strong>Developing Future-Ready Leaders in Southeast Asia</strong>
</p>
<p>The <strong>Southeast Asia Immersion Leadership (SAIL) Programme</strong> is
a prestigious award programme designed to <strong>cultivate a new generation of leaders with a deep understanding of the region</strong>.</p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 100%" height="auto" width="100%" alt="" src="/images/SAIL/SAIL003.jpg">
</div>
<h4><strong>Key Features:</strong></h4>
<ul>
<li>
<p><strong>Twin-City Learning Experience</strong>: A <strong>14-day</strong> immersion
in two Southeast Asian cities.
<br>
<br>
</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Regional Awareness</strong>: Explore socio-economic megatrends
and sustainable urban solutions.
<br>
<br>
</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Hackathon Challenge</strong>: Collaborate with international peers
to develop innovative solutions.
<br>
<br>
</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Industry Networking</strong>: Engage with business and community
leaders across the region.
<br>
<br>
</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Selection Criteria:</strong>
</p>
<ul>
<li>
<p>Strong interest in Southeast Asia (prior OIP/YEP participation preferred).
<br>
<br>
</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Minimum GPA of 3.0 and a demonstrated leadership track record.
<br>
<br>
</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Successful completion of a selection interview.
<br>
<br>
</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Be part of the SAIL experience and lead the way in shaping Southeast Asia’s future.</strong>
