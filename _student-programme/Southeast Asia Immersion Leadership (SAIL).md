<p>The SAIL Programme offers a unique opportunity for <strong>30</strong> students
from Ngee Ann Polytechnic and students from our partner universities in
Southeast Asia, to engage in a two-week immersion experience.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>
<p>Students will travel to 2-cities in Southeast Asia to explore its cultural,
economic, and social dynamics.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>
<p>The programme aims to encourage collaboration through a Twin-City Hackathon,
where participants will work together to solve real-world challenges using
Human-Centred Design Thinking principles.&nbsp;
<br>
<br>This is an Award Programme.</p>
<p>Pre-requisite for this programme:&nbsp;</p>
<ul>
<li>
<p>Applicants must have gone for at least ONE <strong>Southeast Asia</strong> OIP/
YEP/ school signature OIPs.&nbsp;</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>Shortlist students will be called for an interview.&nbsp;SAILwill take
place in Sep vacation annually.&nbsp;Application Period will be Mid-April
to Mid-May</p>
