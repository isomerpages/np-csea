<h2><strong>Preparing Our Educators for the Southeast Asian Landscape</strong></h2>
<p>At Ngee Ann Polytechnic, we believe our educators must be at the forefront
of Southeast Asian business practices and cultural understanding to effectively
prepare our students for regional opportunities. Our comprehensive Staff
Programme offers three distinct pathways to immerse yourself in the dynamic
Southeast Asian environment.</p>
<h4><strong>Programme Highlights:</strong></h4>
<ul>
<li>
<p><strong>Cross-Border Learning</strong>: Gain first-hand exposure to key
industries and innovation hubs.
<br>
<br>
</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Leadership &amp; Growth</strong>: Engage in thought-provoking
discussions on education and workforce trends.
<br>
<br>
</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Networking &amp; Collaboration</strong>: Build connections with
regional experts and institutions.
<br>
<br>
</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Cultural Insights</strong>: Experience the vibrancy of Southeast
Asia through curated visits and activities.
<br>
<br>
</p>
</li>
</ul>
<h2><strong>Programme Options</strong></h2>
<h3><strong>7-Day Twin-City Experience</strong></h3>
<p>A concentrated introduction to two vibrant Southeast Asian business hubs.
Engage with local businesses, educational institutions, and cultural experiences
that will broaden your perspective on regional opportunities.</p>
<h3><strong>3-Week Single-City Immersion</strong></h3>
<p>Deepen your understanding of Southeast Asian business practices through
an extended stay in one regional hub. This programme allows you to:</p>
<ul>
<li>
<p>Strengthen school-based relationships in the region</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Develop networks with potential partners</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Integrate Southeast Asian business practices into teaching and learning</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Support NP's student internship initiatives in the region</p>
</li>
</ul>
<h3><strong>5-Month Industry Attachment</strong></h3>
<p>Our premier staff development opportunity places you directly within a
Southeast Asian organization for genuine industry experience. This comprehensive
programme enables you to:</p>
<ul>
<li>
<p>Build expertise in Southeast Asian business practices</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Develop potential new regional partners for NP</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Identify growth opportunities for NP's presence in the region</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Cultivate strategic relationships with key stakeholders</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Champion NP's Southeast Asia initiatives upon your return</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Create meaningful integration of Southeast Asian perspectives into your
teaching</p>
</li>
</ul>
<h2><strong>Programme Outcomes</strong></h2>
<p>Through these experiences, staff members will:</p>
<ul>
<li>
<p>Gain exposure to and appreciation of Southeast Asian business environments</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Build networks with potential regional partners</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Cultivate an innovative and entrepreneurial mindset</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Learn practical approaches to infuse Southeast Asian business practices
into teaching and learning</p>
</li>
</ul>
<h2><strong>Engagement Activities</strong></h2>
<p>Participants will experience a diverse range of activities:</p>
<ul>
<li>
<p>Corporate visits to local enterprises, MNCs, Singapore enterprises, and
startups/VCs</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Learning sessions with business leaders</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Networking opportunities with regional professionals</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Engagement with local students</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Cultural immersion experiences</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>Join us in becoming Southeast Asia Ready—preparing yourself and our students
for the opportunities beyond Singapore's shores.</p>
<p>This programme is <strong>by nomination</strong> and is tailored for staff
keen on <strong>enhancing their regional expertise and professional development</strong>.&nbsp;</p>
